Today, Ghent holds no grand monument to Rizal—unlike the many statues and plazas in the Philippines bearing his name—but the city remains a quiet witness to a crucial period in the hero’s life. A commemorative plaque was once suggested to mark his residence and honor the significance of his time in Belgium, but such an initiative remains more of a cultural memory than a tourist highlight.

Still, for those who follow the trail of Rizal’s European journey, Ghent offers a unique and intimate glimpse into the sacrifices and solitude behind a revolutionary legacy.

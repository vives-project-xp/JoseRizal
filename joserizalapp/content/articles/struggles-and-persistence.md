title: Struggles and Persistence
preview: Despite financial hardship in Ghent, Rizal persisted in publishing El Filibusterismo, thanks in part to a friend's help.

Rizal’s time in Ghent was marked by hardship. With limited funds, he had difficulty paying the printers, and at one point, the printing process had to be halted. But thanks to the support of his friend Valentin Ventura, who later covered the cost, the novel was completed and published in September 1891.

*El Filibusterismo*, printed in Ghent, would go on to play a major role in fueling the Filipino nationalist movement against Spanish colonial rule.
